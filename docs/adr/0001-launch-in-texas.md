# Launch in Texas

v1 serves Dealers in Texas only. Texas has one Title Manual as its State Ruleset, requires every Dealer to file through webDEALER (since 2025-07-01), and shows Rejections in the portal with a reason and date. Georgia (mailed Rejection letters, paper filing allowed for the smallest Dealers, certified-vendor access) and Florida (state contract and $2M bond) were the alternatives.

Texas access for a non-DMS provider and the county title-service licence question are unresolved. If TxDMV denies Web Service Access and counsel says the licence is needed where most target Dealers are, fall back to Georgia through a certified-vendor partner.
